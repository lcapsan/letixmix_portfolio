Zoo Salvaje

CLIENT

-

YEAR

2016

WORK

Branding, editorial and illustration

DESCRIPTION

Development of the corporate identity and the animal book for Zoo Salvaje. The brand was born with the aim of representing the wild at first sight, making use of the most iconic textures of wild animals: snake, cheetah and zebra. Illustrated freehand for children aged 8 to 14, with vivid and unconventional colors so the book appeals to all audiences, not just kids.

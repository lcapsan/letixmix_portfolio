Man of Steel

CLIENT

-

YEAR

2016

WORK

Posters, illustration and retouch

DESCRIPTION

Design of alternative posters for the film Man of Steel. The commercial poster enhances the sense of heat and fire that runs through the war scenes, using warm tones applied consistently to the protagonist. The teaser posters allude to Superman's sacrifice in defense of Earth, creating a visual link between the two. The alternative poster explores the protagonist's double life and his role as protector of the city.
